DROP-IN FOOTAGE SLOTS
=====================

Put your real footage here with these exact names — no code changes needed:

  hero.mp4   -> replaces the procedural sky film in ACT 1 (fullscreen, muted loop)
  door.mp4   -> replaces the cobalt rift with a video doorway into ACT 2

If a file is missing, the site silently falls back to its procedural visuals.
Videos play muted + looping; audio (if any) can be layered later.
